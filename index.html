Indigenous Incarceration & Social Justice

Examining systemic disparities and outcomes

Figure 8: Indigenous vs Non-Indigenous Imprisonment

The gap continues to widen: Indigenous imprisonment rates surged 35% since 2015, while non-Indigenous rates fell 6%.

Figure 9: Deaths in Custody (2020-2024)

Male deaths in custody significantly outnumber female deaths across all years.

Figure 10: Home Protection Methods (2024)

Deadlocks and security screens are the most common home protection measures across Australia.

Systemic Issue

Indigenous Australians are 14 times more likely to be imprisoned than non-Indigenous Australians, despite making up only 3.8% of the population. This over-representation has increased over the past decade.
